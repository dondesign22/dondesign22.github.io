Privacy Policy — Donaus Music Uploader (ดลอัส มิวสิก)

Last updated: 13 October 2026

What this tool is

"Donaus Music Uploader" is a private, internal command-line tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel ดลอัส มิวสิก (Donaus Music) to publish and manage that channel's own original music videos. It is not distributed to, or usable by, anyone else.

Google / YouTube data we access

The tool uses the YouTube Data API with OAuth 2.0 to access only the channel owner's own YouTube account. It can: upload videos, edit video titles/descriptions/thumbnails/privacy, manage the channel's playlists, and read the channel's own video status and view statistics.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used solely to upload and organise the owner's own songs on the owner's own channel.
  • The OAuth token and a small list of uploaded video IDs are stored locally on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any third party, sold, or used for advertising.
  • No data about other YouTube users, viewers, or channels is collected.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account → Security → Third-party access.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
