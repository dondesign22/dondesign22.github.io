Donaus Music Uploader

A private, internal command-line tool used by the independent Thai music label ดลอัส มิวสิก (Donaus Music) to publish and manage its own original songs on its own YouTube channel: youtube.com/@donausmusic.

Uses YouTube API Services

What it does

  • Uploads the label's own full-length music videos and Shorts (original songs only).
  • Sets titles, descriptions, thumbnails, privacy status and playlists on the label's own channel.
  • Reads the channel's own video status and view counts to track what has been published.

Who uses it

Only the channel owner. The tool is not distributed, sold, or offered to any other person or channel. It signs in to the owner's Google account with OAuth 2.0 (scopes: youtube, youtube.upload).
